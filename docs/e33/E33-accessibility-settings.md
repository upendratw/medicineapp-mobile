# E33 Accessibility Settings

MED-1328 provides elderly-first text-size, contrast, reduced-motion, larger-control, screen-reader-help, reminder-emphasis, and optional haptic preferences. Text remains scalable and wrapping, controls remain at least 48 px (60 px in larger-control mode), and statuses retain words rather than relying on color or haptics. The provider also observes the platform reduced-motion preference. These settings change presentation only; they never change medication or reminder logic.
