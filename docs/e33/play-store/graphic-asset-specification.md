# Google Play Graphic Asset Specification

Design work remains pending. Reverify every requirement against Play Console before upload.

| Asset              | Draft requirement                                                                          |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Store icon         | 512 × 512 px, 32-bit PNG, no invented medical/regulatory badge                             |
| Feature graphic    | 1024 × 500 px JPEG or 24-bit PNG; simple MedicineApp identity and no treatment claim       |
| Phone screenshots  | Portrait, synthetic content, 320–3840 px accepted range; provide device-realistic captures |
| Adaptive icon      | Preserve separate foreground/background/monochrome sources and safe-zone legibility        |
| In-app icon/splash | Preserve current identity; visually review density, contrast, crop, and startup rendering  |

Current source assets are under `assets/images/`. Final assets require design, brand, accessibility, product, and policy review. Do not add approval seals, clinician endorsements, FDA claims, or India regulatory badges.
