# Google Play Screenshot Plan

Use portrait phone captures with synthetic/demo identities and medication data only. Never show real phone numbers, prescriptions, health identifiers, tokens, OTPs, or backend diagnostics. Capture login, patient dashboard, medicine list, schedule/reminder, medication history, drug information, accessibility settings, Hindi UI, and—only with an authorized synthetic scenario—caregiver dashboard.

Suggested captions should describe visible behavior without clinical claims: “Your medication routine at a glance,” “Record what you took,” “Information from approved sources,” and “Adjust text and accessibility preferences.” Keep text readable, high contrast, away from device cutouts, and consistent across localized assets.

Google Play currently accepts phone screenshots from 320–3840 px, with each side no more than twice the other; exact count, device coverage, and current requirements must be reverified in Play Console immediately before submission. No screenshot was generated in this task because no emulator/device was available.
