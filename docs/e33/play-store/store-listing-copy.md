## Full description

MedicineApp is an accessible medication-management companion for adults and authorized caregivers. It helps users organize medicines and schedules, view reminders, record self-reported Taken or Skipped actions, and review medication history.

The app includes foundations for medicine-package and prescription capture with mandatory review before information can be saved. Medication information is supplied by the MedicineApp backend from approved sources. English (India) and Hindi interface foundations and configurable accessibility controls are included.

Some integrations remain under development, including production notification delivery, prescription processing, symptom assessment, and voice transcription. MedicineApp does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend medication changes, dispatch emergency services, or replace a doctor or pharmacist. Availability depends on an authorized MedicineApp account and deployed backend services.
