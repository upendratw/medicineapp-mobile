# MED-0807 Slice 3 mobile Privacy Center

MedicineApp Mobile exposes an authenticated, accessible Privacy Center for Patients and Caregivers. It provides a self-scoped data export, existing correction and caregiver-sharing navigation, bounded erasure status, the established destructive account-deletion action, and the versioned English/Hindi draft privacy notice.

The screen does not imply that live erasure is full erasure. It explains the post-deactivation status limitation and marks symptom correction as policy pending. Account deletion requires a separate destructive confirmation.

Just-in-time notices are placed immediately before or alongside OCR upload, symptom reporting, verified-email setup, caregiver invitation/permission sharing, and notification enablement. The notices contain no patient data and do not claim legal approval or DPDP compliance.

The notice remains pending independent Privacy/Legal review. No native dependency, native configuration, database migration, build, install, provider call, or physical test is introduced by this slice.
