# iOS support checklist

- Verify application identity, EAS project, profile, environment, and backend HTTPS URL.
- Record Xcode/runtime/device/build version actually used.
- Validate launch/auth/onboarding/dashboards, Keychain/logout, camera denial/recovery, push permission/APNs registration, protected deep links, offline recovery, accessibility, E22, and safety prohibitions.
- Distinguish Expo Go, simulator, installed development, preview, TestFlight, and production builds.
- Check content-free logging/telemetry and known limitations.
- Escalate signing, App Store, privacy/legal, clinical/regulatory, incident, and rollback decisions to their owners.
- Never request or print secrets, tokens, signing material, or health data.
