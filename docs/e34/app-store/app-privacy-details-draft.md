# App Privacy details — DRAFT

PRIVACY/LEGAL APPROVAL PENDING. App behavior includes account authentication, user-provided medication/health information sent to the HTTPS backend, optional camera capture initiated by the user, notification permission/token registration, device identifiers for registered delivery, and content-free operational diagnostics. Camera images are not automatically uploaded. Mobile does not directly access MySQL, S3, Gemini, OpenSearch, or AWS credentials.

Before App Store entry, the owner/privacy reviewer must reconcile backend retention, account deletion, linked identifiers, diagnostics, third-party Expo SDK behavior, tracking status, and every collected-data purpose against the then-current Apple questionnaire.
